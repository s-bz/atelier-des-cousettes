## 1. Executive summary

**Site-wide impressions are up, clicks are down, and the gap is explained by two converging local-visibility problems, not by content quality.** Total impressions rose 1,422 → 1,503 (+5.7%) over the last 28 days, but clicks fell 44 → 32 (−27%), so blended CTR dropped from 3.1% to 2.1%. Two findings explain almost all of that gap:

1. **The branded-query CTR collapse flagged last run has gotten dramatically worse.** Combined impressions for "l'atelier des cousettes" / "atelier des cousettes" / "avis sur l'atelier des cousettes" and spelling variants rose 95 → 148 (+56%), but clicks on those exact-brand searches **collapsed from 21 to 4** — a CTR crash from 22.1% to 2.7%. The query is now fragmented across **seven** different pages (was four in October), including nonsensical ranking targets like `/conditions/` (position 60) for the brand name itself. This is the same root cause flagged every run since August (`couture-tarn.fr` still listed as a live, separate Search Console property; the Change of Address has never been registered) — but it was previously a slow-building nuisance. It is now actively costing clicks on the site's own name.
2. **L'Atelier des Cousettes no longer appears in any live SERP pull for its two most important catchment queries.** Three independent DataForSEO pulls this run — "couturiere castres" and "cours de couture castres" in Castres, "cours de couture revel" in Revel — all show **zero appearance** in either the local pack or the organic top 20-29. Last run we held local-pack rank 7 in Castres (a 12-listing pack) and were already confirmed absent in Revel. This run, the business is absent from **every pull in both cities**, not just Revel. GSC's own data corroborates it: "couturiere castres" impressions fell to 1 (was 6, was 12 two runs ago) and "cours de couture revel" still shows zero GSC impressions for a third consecutive window.

**The single most important action, for the second run running, is owner follow-through outside this repo**: register the Change of Address for `couture-tarn.fr` → `atelier-des-cousettes.fr` in Search Console Settings (the 301 redirects are correctly live at the HTTP level — confirmed this run — the problem is purely that Google still treats them as two properties), and check the Google Business Profile listing directly from Castres and Revel to rule out a suspension or a category/attribute change, since DataForSEO's simulated searches no longer find it at all. See §2 and §3.

**This run's repo-side action**: four internal links added from well-indexed blog posts to four glossary pages that regressed from "Discovered — currently not indexed" to **"URL is unknown to Google"** this cycle (worse than last run, not better) — the same proven fix that recovered two orphaned pages in September/October. See §4.
